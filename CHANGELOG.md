# Changelog

## 2026-10-30 — The video fits above the sheet + the Reels bottom band cleared (bundle 0.17.9)
- Extended (no new patch; it extends "Split the screen half and half when comments open on Reels"): capping the sheet at half the screen still left the video the size Facebook made it — full-screen behind the sheet, its lower half hidden, the space above the sheet not the video's own. The comment sheet fragment's own start and view destruction are now hooked, and while a sheet is open the extension settles the playing video's container into exactly the region the sheet leaves: the page's whole width, from the page's top down to the sheet's top edge. The player re-fits the whole frame inside that region, centred, at the largest size its aspect allows — no part of the frame under the sheet, no dead black of the old full-screen container around it — and closing the sheet gives the container back exactly as it was found, so repeated opens and closes do not drift. The container is found by shape, not by name: the largest texture/surface view showing in the window, climbed to while its parents hold nothing but the player. An inline feed video or a thumbnail is never touched, and a sheet open in landscape is left alone. The Hook diagnostics row gains the fit's measures: the container's height before and after, and the region's height.
- Extended (no new patch; it rides "Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab" and the more see-through tab bar switch): the black band at the bottom of the Reels page is cleared. On the Reels tab the tab bar's background fills are now fully transparent instead of half (the switch's way on the other tabs is unchanged, and the bar's icons keep their own paint), and the opaque colour backgrounds of the tab pager, of the Reels page inside it and of the frame the bar stands in are taken off while the tab is picked and given back when it is left. Under them, the reel's video container is lengthened down to the page's bottom edge — ancestors that bind it lengthened with it — so the video runs to the bottom of the screen under the bar instead of stopping above a black band. The diagnostics row gains the clearing's state line and the last gap measured between a reel's container and its page's bottom.
- All 98 patches from the previous build are otherwise unchanged.

## 2026-10-30 — The hunt for the live comment surface + Reels header hides (bundle 0.17.8)
- Extended (no new patch; it extends "Split the screen half and half when comments open on Reels"): the Hook diagnostics row in 0.17.7 settled the question the GĐ9 APK diff had opened — on the test phone the whole in-place split screen path (the controller the ratio and pixel caps stand on) is never reached at all: zero calls while real comments were opened on a reel and on a feed video, the tab bar hooks beside them called 48 times. Which compiled-in surface an account's comments open through is the server configuration's choice, so the half cap now also stands on the other surfaces, each reporting under its own anchor name. On the regular comment sheet: the share of the screen's height is capped at a half where the sheet's parameters are built (the same constructor the full height patch raises a share in), and the window height is capped at a half at both places the popover fragment settles it — the share-times-screen sizing and the fixed eight-tenths box a full share falls back to, the box matching what the phone showed. On the video platform's own split screen (Watch/shorts, comments plugged in through its CommentsViewProvider): the ratio the fragment is created with is watched, and the pixel height it is turned into is capped at a half where it is handed to the sheet's Litho component; the matching overlay height is watched only. Sightings only: each of the five launchers that decide between the split screen and the regular sheet reports that it ran, and so does the set-up of a comment sheet's views. The Hook diagnostics row is extended with what the hunt last saw — the popover share, the split ratio, and the pixel heights before/after each cap — so a phone says which surface its comments really open through.
- Fixed (no new patch; it rides "Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab"): giving the pager's space back no longer stands on the pager's init registration, which the diagnostics row showed never once ran on the test phone even as the bar hid seven times. On every layout pass that leaves the bar hidden for the Reels tab, the live pager is now found from the tab bar's own tree and its bottom padding taken again, and any page padding with it, each real taking counted and the measured values shown on the diagnostics row — so a padding Facebook settles back after the bar hides is taken on the same pass that would draw it.
- New switch (no new patch; it rides the tab bar patch, like the see-through background): "Hide the Reels header" — the Reels page's own header, the strip carrying the word "Reels" and the camera, search and more buttons, is hidden while the Reels tab is showing and its height collapsed to nothing, so reels get that space too; a touch on the surface brings it back for three seconds, the way the tab bar returns; leaving the tab restores it. Found on the live pager page at layout time — by the "Reels" title's row first, then by shape — since the header is a Litho cell with no view of its own to hook. On by default in this build; switch in Hushfacebook settings > Appearance.
- All 98 patches from the previous build are otherwise unchanged.

## 2026-10-30 — Hook diagnostics row (bundle 0.17.7)
- New row (no new patch): "Hook diagnostics", under Hushfacebook settings > Pause, backup and diagnostics. The GĐ9 APK diff proved a phone's own Manager build carries every GĐ5–GĐ8 hook exactly as the tested reference build does, yet the features do nothing on that phone; the hooks' invoked/bound/counted records in HookStatus are what separates "Facebook never reached the hook" from "the hook ran but changed nothing" from "something below undid it" — and until now they were only readable in the exported report file. The row shows them on screen for the three hooks (video top, half-and-half Reels comments, Reels tab bar), plus the tab pager's bottom padding in pixels before and after the last reclaim and after it was restored, the last of those recorded new in the extension at reclaim/restore time. A tap refreshes. Diagnostic only: all 98 patches are otherwise unchanged from 0.17.6.

## 2026-10-30 — Reels black band fixed: the tab pager's own padding (bundle 0.17.6)
- Fix (no new patch; it rides "Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab"): the black band as tall as the tab bar that stayed at the bottom of the Reels tab while the bar was hidden is gone. Re-checked from the beginning on stock 584, the band was the tab pager's own bottom padding: as the pager is built (its init, traced "TabBarViewPager.init") it pads itself by the tab bar's height (56dp, or 44dp on the denser setting), and every page rests inside that padding, so a reel page's bottom edge — its progress bar with it — stops that far above the bottom of the window. Hiding the bar now also lifts the pager's bottom padding, and showing the bar gives the remembered padding back; the feed never showed the band because it scrolls under Facebook's own scroll-away insets. A new hook at the start of the pager's init registers the pager with the extension; the hook was verified in the patched dex.
- All 98 patches from the previous build are otherwise unchanged.
