## 2026-10-30 — Next and previous buttons inside the system PiP window, and the keyboard push waits for a settled height (bundle 0.17.18)
- New patch "Next and previous buttons in the picture-in-picture window" (on by default, in the Playback settings): the floating window a video keeps playing in over other apps is Android's own picture-in-picture, and the one say an app has in it is the action list inside the window's params — so the round mapped that road whole before touching it. Every video surface in this build (the reels viewer, the Watch topic feed, the video tab, and the reels inbox host directly) has its params built with one shared action list, answered by a single factory (LX/dak; in classes15) as [skip back ten seconds, play or pause, skip forward ten seconds], each a broadcast answered by the PiP receiver delegate (LX/eBU;) on the playing video's state manager (LX/55R; in the Groot registry): seek ten seconds either way, play, pause. The patch stands at that factory's returns and appends two actions of its own after Facebook's three, in Facebook's order, untouched: a ▲ for the next video and a ▼ for the one before, their icons drawn at run time, their broadcasts the extension's own, answered by a receiver the extension registers as a session's list is built.
- There is no next-video call to make: the state registry knows only the video playing now — one state manager per video, created and dropped by the surface's own pager as its pages turn — and neither it, nor the playback channel, nor the PiP util's session state offers an advance, a queue or a neighbour; on these surfaces "next" is the pager's own page turn, the turn a swipe makes. So a tap on a switch button keeps the host activity the factory was built with, finds its window's video pager (the largest vertically scrolling recycler in it) and scrolls it one page; the pager's own page-selected work, and the PiP re-arm that follows a new video, are stock's from there. The verdict is read back off the pager's first visible page after the settle — moved is a switch, unmoved a refusal — and the family's block on the Hook diagnostics row carries the host activity the factory last named, the row's length before and after the two buttons, and whether the tap receiver stands registered, with every tap and every refusal counted under its own name, so one screenshot separates a factory that never ran, a receiver that never heard, and a pager that would not turn.
- The platform keeps its own say in the row: Android draws the window and may cap how many of the list's actions it shows. This build never removes or reorders a Facebook action to make room — Facebook's three stay first — so if the phone's cap leaves the added pair out, the diagnostics still show the row built at five, and which stock action to trade for room is a later round's choice, taken with that evidence in hand.
- Fixed (it extends "Post comments at 80 percent, above the keyboard"): on the test phone the 0.17.17 hand push worked but flickered for the whole of the keyboard's slide — the diagnostics counted 269 pushes and the height that stuck was a mid-slide 202px — because the push chased every inset delivery, and a keyboard delivers its height frame by frame as it slides, each frame standing the window somewhere new. A delivery now only nominates the height it carries: a height is applied, counted and told to the video fit only once it has settled — the same value, within a couple of pixels, delivered again, or standing unchanged for a breath (~200ms) — and a settled height moves a standing push only when it differs from the applied one by a real step (over 24px: an emoji panel's height, not a wobble). Zero, the keyboard gone, is held to the strictest reading — repeated and aged — because a push's own geometry change can report a phantom zero for a frame, and an echo of the push itself is not a new keyboard state. The push count is now of settled pushes executed, the row's keyboard heights are the settled ones, and the raw deliveries are counted apart, so the next screenshot tells a keyboard that never settled from one never seen. The video fit's keyboard inset is fed the same settled heights only, so the frame above the sheet no longer breathes with the slide either.

## 2026-10-30 — Swipe the mini player to switch videos (bundle 0.17.12)
- New patch: "Swipe up or down on the mini player to switch videos". A hard fling up on the player's body now plays the next video in its chain and a hard fling down goes back to the one before, the way swiping switches videos in the full player. The gesture is judged at the head of the player's own gesture listener, against the listener's own dismiss threshold (the device's minimum fling velocity scaled by fourteen, read off the listener rather than invented) and only when the fling travels at least twice as far vertically as horizontally, so a diagonal dismiss is never mistaken for a switch; anything weaker, and everything while the switch is off, falls through to the stock snap and dismiss untouched, and dragging the player to move it and tapping it for its controls are never hooked at all. The switch itself is never the extension's own work: it calls the controller's stock advance — the primitive stock calls when a chained video ends — which polls the chaining session's queue and builds the next player through the stock factory, signal and all. Going back, and going forward again over ground already covered, first puts the wanted story back at the head of that queue, so the swap that follows is stock's own in every part. The way back is a history the extension keeps itself, one per mini player session, because stock keeps none: a hook at the head of the advance primitive notes the outgoing story and the story at the head of the queue before every swap, so autoplay's advances move the pointer exactly as the extension's own do, an advance nobody predicted is reconciled against the player's real story at the next gesture, and new ground taken from the middle of the history drops the redone tail. Nothing can strand the player: a chaining session the server never gave the controller (chaining is a server choice), an empty queue at the head of the history, the start of the history, a queue that cannot take a story back, or an advance that changed nothing each refuse the fling — the stock snap or dismiss runs instead — and leave their own counted line on the Hook diagnostics row, which also gains the family's block and a sticky line saying whether a chaining session was seen at all and the queue's size then, so a phone whose account has no chaining says so outright instead of the feature failing silently.
