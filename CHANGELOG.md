# Changelog

## 2026-10-30 — Swipe the mini player to switch videos (bundle 0.17.12)
- New patch: "Swipe up or down on the mini player to switch videos". Stock, the small floating player a video shrinks into answers a tap with its control bar and a fling only by snapping into a corner or dismissing — there is no way to reach another video without opening the player back up, and nothing anywhere remembers the video before. A hard fling up on the player's body now plays the next video in its chain and a hard fling down goes back to the one before, the way swiping switches videos in the full player. The gesture is judged at the head of the player's own gesture listener, against the listener's own dismiss threshold (the device's minimum fling velocity scaled by fourteen, read off the listener rather than invented) and only when the fling travels at least twice as far vertically as horizontally, so a diagonal dismiss is never mistaken for a switch; anything weaker, and everything while the switch is off, falls through to the stock snap and dismiss untouched, and dragging the player to move it and tapping it for its controls are never hooked at all. The switch itself is never the extension's own work: it calls the controller's stock advance — the primitive stock calls when a chained video ends — which polls the chaining session's queue and builds the next player through the stock factory, signal and all. Going back, and going forward again over ground already covered, first puts the wanted story back at the head of that queue, so the swap that follows is stock's own in every part. The way back is a history the extension keeps itself, one per mini player session, because stock keeps none: a hook at the head of the advance primitive notes the outgoing story and the story at the head of the queue before every swap, so autoplay's advances move the pointer exactly as the extension's own do, an advance nobody predicted is reconciled against the player's real story at the next gesture, and new ground taken from the middle of the history drops the redone tail. Nothing can strand the player: a chaining session the server never gave the controller (chaining is a server choice), an empty queue at the head of the history, the start of the history, a queue that cannot take a story back, or an advance that changed nothing each refuse the fling — the stock snap or dismiss runs instead — and leave their own counted line on the Hook diagnostics row, which also gains the family's block and a sticky line saying whether a chaining session was seen at all and the queue's size then, so a phone whose account has no chaining says so outright instead of the feature failing silently. On by default; switch in Hushfacebook settings > Playback.
- All 98 patches from the previous build are otherwise unchanged.

And it is no longer silent when it fails: the diagnostics row gains the hunt's own line — how many tries it made, the largest surface it met, and why it stopped (no surface in the window, the largest too small, a surface found but its container not settled, a landscape window).

The pass had silent doors: it returned without a state whatever part of its answer failed, and its clearing only took a plain colour background, passing every other kind by. Every way out of the pass now leaves its reason as the state — the patch not in the build, the settings not ready, not the Reels tab, the more see-through switch off, no tab pager found from the bar. The clearing takes any background at all: mutated first so a drawable Facebook shares changes only here, remembered with the alpha it was found with and given back on leaving the tab, and the targets add the tab bar's direct ancestors on the way up to the window. ## 2026-10-30 — Reels black band fixed: the tab pager's own padding (bundle 0.17.6)
- Fix (no new patch; it rides "Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab"): the black band as tall as the tab bar that stayed at the bottom of the Reels tab while the bar was hidden is gone. Re-checked from the beginning on stock 584, the band was the tab pager's own bottom padding: as the pager is built (its init, traced "TabBarViewPager.init") it pads itself by the tab bar's height (56dp, or 44dp on the denser setting), and every page rests inside that padding, so a reel page's bottom edge — its progress bar with it — stops that far above the bottom of the window. Hiding the bar now also lifts the pager's bottom padding, and showing the bar gives the remembered padding back; the feed never showed the band because it scrolls under Facebook's own scroll-away insets. A new hook at the start of the pager's init registers the pager with the extension; the hook was verified in the patched dex.
