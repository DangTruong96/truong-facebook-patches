## 2026-10-31 — Nút chuyển video PiP: giữ trang bằng sự kiện của chính pager (bundle 0.17.22)

- Sửa tận gốc lỗi "2 nút không hoạt động" của bản 0.17.21: pager bắt giữ được trên bề mặt Reel không phải RecyclerView như bản đồ tĩnh của đợt trước dự đoán, mà là pager dọc của chính Facebook (lớp bị đổi tên X.3xR, kế thừa ViewPager). Phép đọc trang cũ dùng hai primitive của RecyclerView — cả hai đều không áp được trên ViewPager (một cái là field số nguyên chứ không phải method, một cái không tồn tại) — nên chết tại mọi lần chạm: 17/17 lần bấm trên máy thật bị từ chối "không đọc được trang của pager". Lỗi mang tính tĩnh, không phải do trạng thái PiP; nó chỉ lộ lúc chạm vì lúc bắt giữ chưa từng thử đọc (số "trang 1" trong chẩn đoán cũ là thứ tự ô con đang gắn của pager, không phải vị trí trang thật).
- Giữ trạng thái trang thay vì đọc lúc chạm: khi bề mặt còn sống ở trạng thái thường, trang hiện tại được gieo từ chính field trạng thái của pager rồi được cập nhật liên tục bằng listener sự kiện chọn trang của chính pager (đăng ký bằng Proxy lên interface listener của bản dựng này); lúc bấm trong PiP, lệnh chuyển trang ±1 đi từ chỉ số đã biết bằng chính method đặt trang của pager, và sự kiện chọn trang tiếp theo (hoặc một lần đọc lại field trạng thái) xác minh kết quả — không còn phụ thuộc bố cục của cửa sổ PiP.
- Chặn biên theo đúng số trang của adapter: bấm nút "kế" ở video cuối cùng bị từ chối đích danh "đã ở video cuối" thay vì gửi cho pager một chỉ số nó không có.
- Bắt giữ pager đáng tin hơn: mọi lần thử đều được đếm (số lần thử / số lần đậu hiện trong trang chẩn đoán kèm lý do trượt gần nhất); lần trượt có thể chỉ vì trang chưa kịp dàn xong (kích thước bằng 0) thì tự thử lại tối đa 2 lần theo nhịp dàn trang của cửa sổ, thay vì chết lặng như đợt trước (chỉ đậu 1 trong 5 lần dựng params mà không ghi lý do nào).
- Chẩn đoán hook có thêm: loại pager đã giữ và con đường biết trang của nó, trang đang biết gần nhất cùng nguồn của nó (sự kiện của pager / số đọc lúc bắt giữ / số đọc sau lần chuyển), và thống kê bắt giữ pager.
- Giữ nguyên: hàng 3 nút PiP [▼ · phát/dừng gốc · ▲] của 0.17.19, cơ chế phục vụ trang Reel hết dải đen của 0.17.20, bàn phím bình luận của 0.17.21 và toàn bộ các patch còn lại; vá thử trên Facebook 584.0.0.51.56: 101 patch áp dụng, 0 lỗi.

## 2026-10-31 — The PiP switch buttons turn the pager captured as the surface armed (bundle 0.17.21)
- Fixed (it extends "Next and previous buttons in the picture-in-picture window"): on the test phone the 0.17.19 row stood exactly as composed — the diagnostics read the row rebuilt four times, the last build 3 before and 3 after, the receiver registered — and the taps arrived and were counted (next ×18, previous ×6), but every one of the 24 switches was refused with "PiP switch refused: no video pager in the window". The recycler's renamed smoothScrollToPosition is told by its body: it hands the turn to the layout manager's `smoothScrollToPosition` — a name the layout manager kept — behind the stock no-layout-manager log; the renamed getChildAdapterPosition reads the position out of the child's view holder, where its sibling reads the layout position out of the same holder. Both are public on the recycler, so the extension reaches them reflectively off whatever concrete pager it captured.
- The tap no longer searches. Every build of the actions factory's list — the hook that already runs as a surface arms, while the surface stands alive at full size — now also captures: the reel page then showing in the host window is located by the reel page serve's own candidate rules (the largest video surface in the window, its container, the climb from the container to the paging parent, kept only when the page answers the full-screen test — page spanning the window, container filling the page — so a feed item's player is never captured), and that page's pager is kept weakly beside the page and the pager's own class name, refreshed at every build so the reference follows the surface as pages and sessions turn. The capture is this family's own walk over the serve's public surface/container finds; the 0.17.20 watcher's state is neither read nor written.
- A tap speaks only to the captured pager. It refuses by name when there is no host, when the host is not in picture-in-picture, when no pager was ever captured, or when the captured pager is no longer attached in the host's window. It reads the showing page — the pager's child overlapping the pager's box the most — and that page's adapter position; a tap back from position 0 is refused as already at the first video. The turn itself is the pager's own smooth scroll to the position one over, counted apart for each direction when sent, and after the settle (the same 1.1s the old judge allowed) the position is read again the same way: a page further along the tap's way is a switch, counted as before; an unmoved pager is the old refusal; a pager or page no longer readable is the old unverified. Each refusal keeps its own counted line, and the family's block on the Hook diagnostics row gains two sticky lines — "PiP switch pager last captured: …" (the pager's class, exactly as this build names it) and "PiP switch pager page last read: …" — beside the counted "PiP video pager captured as the surface armed", so one screenshot after a few taps tells the whole story: whether a pager was ever captured, which one, what page it stood on, and where a switch stopped.
- The three-button row of 0.17.19 is untouched — the same hook at the same two returns of the one factory, the factory's own play or pause object carried over whole, the seeks given up only in the window and only while the switch is on — and the reel page serve of 0.17.20 is untouched. No new patch: the bundle still carries 101 patches, all others unchanged. Checked in a test patch run (101 patches applied, none failed, the APK signed with the project certificate) and in the patched build's own code — the extension's new counted strings stand in the patched dex and the old window-search refusal is gone from it; not yet on a phone.

## 2026-10-31 — The Reels bottom lengthening serves each page as it takes the screen (bundle 0.17.20)
- Fixed (no new patch; it extends "Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab" and the comments video fit's bottom work): on the test phone the bottom lines of the Hook diagnostics row read "not recorded" through three builds (0.17.16–0.17.18) — with tab family counts in the hundreds beside them — while the first video of a Reels session filled the screen and every video after it stood a black band short at the bottom for its whole length. The static diagnosis names two shapes starving the old pass together. Its only driver was the tab bar container's own layout hook (through the bottom clearing), which fires around tab entry and touch-hold show/hide cycles, never when a reel page turns — so no pass ever visited the pages after the first. And the locate both its measure and its lengthening shared, `locateReelContainer` → `findVideoSurface` from the window's decor, walked the whole window on a fixed 60,000-node budget with no geometry pruning, descending every visible subtree — the other tab pages the tab pager keeps alive beside Reels, the reel pager's own off-screen pages — so the budget spent itself in the sibling trees before the player was reached (the 0.17.11 note had already caught a smaller budget dying "in the page's tree before the player"); both callers then returned writing nothing — no sticky field, no counted line, no note — and the chain climb itself had a silent exit when it found no short box. Hence three screenshots of "not recorded" after any amount of Reels browsing.
- The lengthening now runs per page. A watcher stands on a window's pre-draws, throttled: a pruned walk (any subtree lying wholly outside the window and carrying no transform is skipped whole; an unpruned fallback walk answers before a window is called empty) finds the showing surface, its container, and its page — the direct child of its paging container, told by superclass name so a RecyclerView reel pager counts, not only the first-generation ViewPager the old page-telling knew, or the window's own child for a full-screen page of its own, qualified as a full-screen reel page by the page spanning the window and the container filling the page (a page standing the band short still qualifies; a feed item never does). Once the same page has stood showing across two walks it is served on its own chain: the page before's lengthening is given back first and the pass re-runs after that layout, so shared ancestors are measured as Facebook left them and never lengthened twice; the container is lengthened to the page's bottom and the chain past the page to the window's with the 0.17.17 hands (margin back, exact height for a box with none of its own, whole chain in one pass). A standing page is re-served only when its gap comes back (three times at most), and leaving the tab or the answer flipping stops the window's watch and restores as before. The watcher is seeded from three hooks that already touch a reel's window — the tab bar container's layout (through the bottom clearing, which no longer calls the lengthening itself), a comment sheet's start in any activity, and the PiP actions factory's host activity — so the full-screen reel viewer opened from the feed or a search is served by the same doorbells, not only the Reels tab.
- Telemetry that cannot stand silent: every serve is counted (in all, and per pager index), and so is a page already full height, a pass that found no showing page, a re-serve, and a page change giving the last page's lengthening back. The tab bar block gains three sticky lines — "Reels page serve: last page … at index …, gap … before, … after" (the after-gap refreshed as layouts land, so a later screenshot reads ≈0), "Reels page serve note", and "Reels page locate" with the walk's node and pruned counts — and the height chain's note now also answers "the page's chain already reaches the window bottom" instead of standing blank. After any Reels browsing, one diagnostics screenshot now shows the passes and their numbers; "not recorded" survives only before the first browsing. No new patch: the bundle still carries 101 patches, and all others are unchanged. Checked in a test patch run (101 patches applied, none failed) and in the patched build's own code — the serve's counted strings and its watcher entry stand in the patched dex; not yet on a phone.
